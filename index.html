Lincoln & The Clay Family

Military Alliance

Davy Crockett

The Connection: Crockett served alongside Williamson and Perry ancestors in the 2nd Regiment of Tennessee Volunteers during the 1813 Creek War. They shared camps and scouting trails across the Coosa River.

Daniel Boone

The Connection: Boone was a scouting partner to the Skaggs and Sellards lines. He famously wintered at the Sellards settlement and utilized the "Warrior's Path" trails co-established by the Perry and Clay hunters.
