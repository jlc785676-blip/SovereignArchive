Section 1: Introduction & Purpose

This document serves as the master genealogical archive of the Chaney family, consolidating all known family history information across multiple bloodlines into a single, comprehensive reference. It has been compiled for Joshua "Josh" Chaney of Marysville, Ohio, and is intended to function as the definitive record of the Chaney ancestry — encompassing the Chaney, Vance, Perry, Clay, Watts, Dyer, and Davis family lines.

The information contained herein has been drawn from verified genealogical records, family tree documentation, oral histories, and ongoing research. Where facts have been confirmed through documentary evidence, they are presented as established record. Where information remains unverified, incomplete, or subject to further investigation, it is clearly marked with "[To Be Confirmed]" or "[Date Unknown]" notations to preserve the integrity of the archive.

Section 3: The Chaney Line

Leonard Chaney (1895–1975) was born on 12 June 1895 and died on 17 September 1975, at the age of eighty. He is the earliest confirmed ancestor bearing the Chaney surname in this family tree and represents the foundation of the documented patrilineal line.

Newman Chaney (d. 2017) was the son of Leonard Chaney and the paternal grandfather of Joshua Chaney. He married Arminda Vance.

Oshel Chaney (b. 1949) was born in 1949, the son of Newman Chaney and Arminda Vance. He married Erma Perry.

Section 4: The Vance Line

John Hobert Vance (1897–1968) was born on 22 March 1897 and died on 10 December 1968. He married Lillie Lily Dyer.

Lillie Lily Dyer (1901–1982) was born on 4 February 1901 and died in November 1982. Her maiden name connects the Vance line to the Dyer family.

Arminda Vance (b. c. 1935) was the daughter of John Hobert Vance and Lillie Lily Dyer. Arminda married Newman Chaney.

Section 5: The Perry Line

Ira Perry (1884–1974) was born on 16 October 1884 and died in February 1974. He is the great-grandfather of Joshua Chaney on the maternal side.

Walter Lake Perry was the son of Ira and Sarah Perry. He married Pansey Perry.

Erma Perry (b. 1955) was the daughter of Walter Lake Perry and Pansey Perry. She married Oshel Chaney.

Section 6: The Clay Line & Yorkshire Clay Lineage

Family research has explored a potential connection between the Thomas John Clay line and earlier Clay families traced to Yorkshire, England. If confirmed, this lineage places the Clay branch within the broader narrative of English migration to the American colonies during the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries.

Section 9: Indigenous Heritage & Relations

Family history discussions have included references to Indigenous heritage within the Perry and Vance lines. This includes the oral histories of a young Shawnee boy adopted into the Perry household, functioning as a "Bridge Family."
